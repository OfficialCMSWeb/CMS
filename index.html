Graduation & Excellence

100% Graduation Rate

For 55 consecutive years, Clara Muhammad School has maintained a 100% graduation rate, with over 90% of alumni advancing to college. Building on a decades-long legacy of shaping Nashville community leaders, our graduates now pioneer and innovate at the forefront of STEM industries nationwide.

Rigorous Curriculum

At Clara Muhammad School, students push past standard limits by mastering accelerated, above-grade-level academics. This rigor is paired with a foundational Islamic education rooted in the teachings of Islam and the life of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him).

Future Leaders

Clara Muhammad School has a rich legacy of cultivating civic and community leaders in Nashville. Today, that tradition extends into STEM, where alumni drive industry innovation and shape the future of their fields.
